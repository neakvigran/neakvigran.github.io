Convert the hexadecimal value to decimal:

`3 x 16^1 + 13 x 16^0 = 61`

Hexadecimal uses base 16, and `d` represents 13. Therefore, `0x3d` equals $$3 \times 16 + 13 = 61$$ in decimal.

`picoctf{61}`
